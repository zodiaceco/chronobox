Chronobox logo variants

New at the top: four small changes to Z3, so that it reads as a sun on its path and not as a ring. Below them are four versions that go further. Then the marks and wordmarks close to the Zodiac logo. Below them are the eleven earlier variations of the mark and three of the wordmark. Each card shows the mark on white and on black, in the header lockup, at 32, 20 and 16 pixels, and as an app icon.
