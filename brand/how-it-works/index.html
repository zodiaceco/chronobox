How it works

Three options for the four steps on the Timebox start page. Each on white and on near-black, at 680 px and at 290 px.

option-a/

Four small pictograms in the mark's vocabulary: a wallet, the sun leaving the horizon, a coin going into a tray, the sun at rest. On a phone they become a list.

Recommendation

Option A. Four separate pictures read at a glance, each step has its own object (wallet, the sun leaving the horizon, a coin, the sun at rest), and it fits both widths without shrinking the art. Option B is the most beautiful at 680 px but its art gets small on a phone, and Option C tells a nice story that ends in the app icon but its four boxes look too alike to scan quickly.
