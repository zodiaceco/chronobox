Recommendation

Two horizons is the one to take: it is the Zodiac horizon put to work as an hourglass, so it says both parent and delay, and it stays clear at 16 px and in one colour. Pair it with the real Zodiac wordmark, a thin divider and TIMEBOX, and use the endorsed version with a small by Zodiac line where space is tight.
